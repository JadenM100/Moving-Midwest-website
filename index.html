GOOD QUESTIONS

you book.

Still have a question? Give us a call.

Can you deliver today?+

Often, yes. Same-day availability depends on our schedule, pickup location, travel distance, appliance size, and delivery details. Call or submit a request and we’ll let you know as quickly as possible.

Do you install appliances?+

We primarily provide pickup, delivery, and placement. Basic plug-in or connection assistance may be available for certain electric appliances. We do not perform licensed plumbing, gas, or electrical work.

How much does delivery cost?+

Pricing depends on distance, appliance size, stairs, access, and any extra help required. Call or submit the form for a fast quote.

What areas do you serve?+

We serve customers throughout our service area in Indiana and Illinois. Contact us with the pickup and delivery locations to confirm availability.
